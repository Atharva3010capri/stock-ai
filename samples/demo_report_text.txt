Nimbus Foods Limited - Annual Report FY2024 (fictional sample for testing)

CHAIRMAN'S OVERVIEW
Nimbus Foods Limited is a packaged foods company headquartered in Pune, India. The Company operates in the Fast Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) industry within the Consumer Staples sector. Our business is organised into two segments: Snacks and Beverages. During FY2024 the Company launched the Nimbus Oat Crunch product range and expanded distribution to 1.2 million retail outlets.

SUBSIDIARIES AND STRUCTURE
Nimbus Foods Limited owns 100% of Nimbus Beverages Private Limited, which manufactures and sells the Company's bottled juice range. Nimbus Foods Limited also holds a 60% stake in Cloudnine Dairy Limited, a subsidiary of Nimbus Foods Limited that supplies milk solids to the Snacks segment. Mr. Arjun Mehta serves as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, and Ms. Priya Nair serves as Chief Financial Officer.

MARKET AND COMPETITION
The Company competes with Zenith Snacks Limited and Harbor Beverages Limited in its core categories. Nimbus Foods sells to modern trade chains including QuickMart and to general trade distributors across India. Palm oil and wheat flour are the Company's largest raw material inputs, and the Company sources palm oil from Indonesian suppliers.

FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE (all figures in INR crore unless stated otherwise)
Revenue from operations for FY2024 was 4,250.0 crore compared with 3,700.0 crore in FY2023, a growth of 14.9%. Operating profit (EBIT) for FY2024 was 637.5 crore against 518.0 crore in FY2023. Net profit for the year (profit after tax) was 425.0 crore in FY2024 compared with 352.0 crore in FY2023. Basic earnings per share were INR 21.25 in FY2024 and INR 17.60 in FY2023.

BALANCE SHEET
Total assets as at 31 March 2024 stood at 5,100.0 crore. Total equity attributable to owners was 2,500.0 crore. Total borrowings (long term and short term) were 750.0 crore. Total current assets were 1,800.0 crore and total current liabilities were 1,200.0 crore.

CASH FLOWS AND DIVIDENDS
Net cash generated from operating activities was 560.0 crore in FY2024. Purchase of property, plant and equipment (capital expenditure) amounted to 210.0 crore. The Board recommended a dividend of INR 8.00 per share for FY2024.

RISK FACTORS
The Company's margins are affected by palm oil prices and wheat prices, which are volatile and influenced by global weather and crude oil prices through biofuel demand. A depreciation of the Indian rupee against the US dollar increases the cost of imported palm oil. Changes in food safety regulation by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India may require product reformulation. The Beverages segment is seasonal and sensitive to summer temperatures.

OUTLOOK
Management expects to focus on premium snacks, expand the Oat Crunch range and reduce borrowings over the next two years. These statements are forward looking and subject to the risks described above.
